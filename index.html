Discovering My Ministry Calling

God has given each of us different gifts, passions, experiences, and opportunities to serve others.

This guide will help you explore where you might fit and what your next step could be.

There are no right or wrong answers. This is simply a way to help you discern your next step.

Step 1

Where are you most interested in serving?

Start with the area where you sense the greatest interest, burden, or desire to participate.

REACH
“Reaching Everyone and Converting Hearts”
GO to the Lost, BRING them to Christ, and JOIN them to the church.
CORE
“Creating Ongoing Renewal Environments”
CARE for members, BUILD them in the faith, and DEPLOY them for ministry.
LEAD
“Leadership Empowerment and Development”
SPOT potential leaders, TRAIN them, ASSIGN them or SEND them out.
ADMIN
“Administering the Development of Ministry Infrastructure and Networks”
Support the ministry through Finance, Facilities, Systems, Operations, and Resources.

Step 3 of 6

What draws you to this?

Think about what makes this area of ministry meaningful or exciting to you.

Step 4 of 6

What strengths can you bring?

Select the strengths that describe you. You may choose more than one.

Step 5 of 6

What can your current season accommodate?

Ministry should fit realistically within the season of life God has given you. This is not a test of commitment.

Step 6 of 7

What do you hope to see happen?

Imagine yourself one year from now. What would make you say, “I believe my service is making a difference”?

Step 7 of 7

How would you like to be involved?

There are different ways to participate in ministry. Choose the kinds of involvement that fit your gifts, availability, and season of life.

You may choose more than one.

Your choices are simply a starting point for prayer, discernment, and conversation.
